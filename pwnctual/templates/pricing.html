Questions

Is the course really free?expand_more
Yes, forever. Every chapter, video, write-up and challenge, plus ranks and the leaderboard, without paying or adding a card. New chapters are free too.
What do I get with Pro?expand_more
1-on-1 calls with a mentor for your questions and doubts. The lessons are the same for everyone; Pro is for when you want to talk something through with a person.
How do the 1-on-1 calls work?expand_more
Pick a free 15-minute slot on the calls page, shown in your timezone, and say what you need help with. You join a video call with a mentor and go through it together. You can hold one booking at a time. Without Pro, you can still ask in the comments under each video.
Do I need anything installed?expand_more
Just a browser and a terminal. Lessons are videos with a written explanation, and the challenges are live machines you connect to (like OverTheWire), so there's nothing to install for the course itself.
