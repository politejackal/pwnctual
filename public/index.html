Stuck?

Stuck? Good. That's learning.

Getting stuck is how learning works in this field. Every hacker spends most of their time stuck; the skill you're building is getting unstuck. Nobody is timing you.

block Please don't search for a walkthrough. It hands you the answer and takes away the lesson: you'd clear the level and learn nothing from it.

Ask in the comments of the lecture video on YouTube instead. Say exactly where you're stuck:

  • which challenge you're on
  • what you're trying to do
  • the commands you tried
  • what happened: the exact error or output

Never post a level's password. We'll reply with a hint that points you the right way without spoiling the challenge, so you still get to solve it yourself and actually learn something.
