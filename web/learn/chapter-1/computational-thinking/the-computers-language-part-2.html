Awesome, now you know how to read a bunch of zeroes and ones. This bunch of ones and zeroes is known as binary. Now it's important for hackers like us to be able to convert a normal number (decimal number) to binary!

Now there is a simple trick to convert a given decimal number to binary. You take the decimal number, for example 21, and check which digit's place is just smaller than it. I see it is the 16's place. So whenever I see a place where my number is bigger than that place's number, I put a 1 over there and subtract the value of that place from my number.

So now from 21, I have 5 remaining. Now what next? Well, I go to the next digit's value, which is 8, but it is bigger than my current value. So I put a zero at that digit's place and move on. Next I see is 4, which is lower than my current decimal value, so I subtract 4 and put a 1 over there. Now I have 1 remaining, and I continue the steps till I have a remainder of zero. If you don't get a remainder of zero, you can safely assume that you did something wrong, and just redo the steps.
