pwnctual is built by me, Polite Jackal, alone. I am a vulnerability research student myself. pwnctual is a course specifically designed to take you from nobody to a specialized vulnerability researcher. Though you can specialize in a specific field like browsers, AI systems, OS kernels etc., the foundation is the same for all vulnerability research fields.

pwnctual is built in a way such that each year acts as a checkpoint, and makes a difference big enough between somebody who has learnt about how to swim and somebody who has swum. Anyway, that's becoming poetic. But that's what it is. All I mean to say is each year makes a difference so big.

pwnctual is designed to be a hands-on course, where you practice everything you learn. So at the end of the day you don't have to worry if you learnt or not, because you can't solve the challenges yourself if you didn't understand a topic!

pwnctual starts from introduction to computer science, all the way till you are ready to find real zero-days.
