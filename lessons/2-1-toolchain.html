In Chapter 1, I said that computers are all ones and zeroes. That’s freaking true! They don’t know what the hell abc is. They just know what electricity flowing and not flowing means.

But in C, we aren’t typing zeroes and ones, so how the hell will the CPU know what we mean? This is where things get interesting, for me at least.

Your C file undergoes lots and lots of modifications until it’s a bunch of zeroes and ones. Yes, that’s the final file, known as the program, and it is a bunch of ones and zeroes.

In this topic, we will be discussing the various processes our C file goes through to give us the ones and zeroes we want.
